# 2027 Medicare Advantage Blackout Dates

**Source:** THEI internal — carrier contracting blackout schedule for 2027 plan year  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-24  
**Applies to:** All internal transfers and hierarchy changes

Agents and agencies cannot transfer or release during the blackout periods listed below. New contracting blackouts are separate from transfer/release blackouts.
